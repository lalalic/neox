Neox Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2025

1. Introduction

Neox ("we", "our", "the App") is an AI-powered agent for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

Data You Provide

  • Chat Messages: Text, voice, and image messages you send to the AI agent.
  • Projects: Project names, notes, and context files you create within the app.
  • Settings: Configuration preferences and API keys you enter.

Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Token consumption and credit balance for your personal dashboard.
  • Purchase Data: Transaction records for in-app purchases (processed by Apple and Stripe).
  • Push Notifications: Device tokens for delivering notifications (managed by Apple).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the AI agent and process your requests
  • To store your projects and conversation history locally on your device
  • To process payments via Apple StoreKit and Stripe
  • To deliver push notifications about task completions

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Conversation history, project files, and settings remain on your iPhone. We do not maintain central servers or databases that store your personal data.

5. Third-Party Services

  • AI Language Model: Your conversations with the AI agent are processed by the configured LLM provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic). These interactions are subject to the provider's privacy policy.
  • Apple StoreKit: In-app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Stripe: Credit purchases processed through Stripe are subject to Stripe's privacy policy.
  • WeChat / Discord: If you connect messaging channels, messages are routed through their respective platforms under their privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy (third-party services above).

7. Data Retention

Your data is stored on your device for as long as you use the app. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Conversations processed by LLM providers are subject to their retention policies.

8. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data at any time through the app
  • Disconnect messaging channels at any time
  • Delete the app to remove all local data

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures. API keys and credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain. All network communications use HTTPS.

10. Children's Privacy

Neox is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact
