Frequently Asked Questions

What is Neox?

Neox is an AI agent for iOS that can chat, browse the web, operate your camera, manage projects, and integrate with messaging platforms like WeChat and Discord.

How do credits work?

Credits are used to process AI requests. Each message consumes tokens based on its length and complexity. You can purchase credits via in-app purchase or Stripe.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Conversations sent to AI models are processed by the configured LLM provider. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use my own API keys?

Yes, you can configure your own LLM provider API keys in the app settings.

How do I connect WeChat or Discord?

Open Settings in the app and configure your messaging channels. Follow the on-screen instructions to authenticate.

How do I request a refund?

For Apple in-app purchases, request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem page. For Stripe purchases, contact us at neox@qili2.com.
